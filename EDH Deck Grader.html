EDH Deck Grader

Players didn't need another score. They needed to know which cards to change.

A Commander deck grader that explains every number and names the cards to swap.

Role Product Designer & Builder
Duration 7 months
Tools Figma, React, FastAPI, Claude Code

TL;DR

Deck tools show what's in your deck, not what to change. I built a grader that sets targets for your commander, shows its math, and names the cards to cut and add.

The Challenge

A 100-card puzzle with no answer key

99

cards plus a commander, no duplicates

32k+

legal cards to choose from

5

power brackets to match your table

Counts, no targets

"9 ramp cards" means nothing without knowing the right number for this commander.

Scores, no reasons

"7/10" doesn't tell you what to edit.

Advice, no cards

"Add more removal" leaves the hard part to you.

What I Learned

Show the target, not just the score.

A visible target turns a low grade into a to-do list.

Silent failures look like bad design.

Players blamed the grader, not the data.

With an AI pair, my job was judgement.

Building got cheap. Deciding what "correct" means didn't.
